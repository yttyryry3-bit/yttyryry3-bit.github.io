Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. What we collect

AutoPost stores OAuth access tokens for the social accounts you connect, on your own device (encrypted storage) and, for TikTok token exchange only, transiently on your own relay server. We operate no central servers and collect no analytics.

2. What we do not collect

We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data. Your videos stay on your device except when you choose to publish them to a platform via its official API.

3. Third parties

When you publish, your video and caption are sent to the platform(s) you selected (YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram) under their privacy policies.

4. Data deletion

Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its tokens from your device. Uninstalling the app removes all local data.

5. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app provider through the support channel listed in the app.
